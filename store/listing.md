## Full description

ECHO helps you look into what is happening to a place on Earth, and be honest about what the evidence does and does not show.

Start an investigation anywhere: use your location, pick a spot on the map, or type coordinates. Then step through dates and slide a before/after comparison across real NASA satellite imagery.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Compare dates: swipe between NASA MODIS or VIIRS true-colour imagery from different days.
• Planet Pulse: browse open natural events reported by NASA EONET (wildfires, storms, volcanoes, floods and more) as a list or on a map, and start an investigation from any of them.
• Keep notes: pin observations to the map, with the date they refer to.
• Ask ECHO: get research guidance for your investigation, from a built-in guide that works offline or from an AI service you choose to connect.
• Export a report or a full backup and share it with the share sheet.
• Works with patchy signal: your investigations live on your phone, and the last set of events is kept for offline reading.

BUILT TO BE HONEST
ECHO shows its sources and its limits. The daily NASA imagery is coarse (roughly 250 to 375 metres per pixel), and clouds can hide the ground. Overlays that are only illustrations are labelled as illustrations. ECHO does not detect floods or deforestation for you and is not for emergency response or operational decisions.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no analytics. Your investigations and notes stay on your phone. Your location is used only when you tap "My location".

DATA
Imagery: NASA GIBS (MODIS Terra and Aqua, VIIRS) and Esri World Imagery. Events: NASA EONET. Map engine: Leaflet.
