# ECHO privacy policy (draft)

Last updated: 2026-10-19

This is a plain-language draft written for the ECHO apps. It describes what the code in this repository does. Before you publish it, fill in the contact details, host it at a public web address, and have it checked against the laws that apply to you. I am not a lawyer and this is not legal advice.

## Who we are
ECHO is published by **[your name or organisation]**. Contact: **[your email address]**.

## What stays on your device
Your investigations, notes, settings and the last list of events are stored on your device only. ECHO has no accounts, no server of its own, and no analytics or advertising tools. We cannot see your investigations or notes.

You can export a backup or a report at any time, and erase everything from Settings > Erase all data. Uninstalling the app also removes the data.

## Location
If you tap "My location", the app asks your device for your current position once and uses it to fill in the coordinates field. The position is not stored except as the coordinates of an investigation you choose to create, and it is not sent to us. You can type coordinates or pick a place on the map instead, and you can refuse the permission.

## Services the app contacts
To show maps and events, the app requests data from these services. Like any website or app that loads content from a server, they receive your device's IP address and ordinary request details, and they can see which map area and date you are viewing. Each has its own privacy policy.
- NASA GIBS (gibs.earthdata.nasa.gov): satellite imagery for the dates you view.
- Esri (server.arcgisonline.com): base satellite imagery and place names.
- NASA EONET (eonet.gsfc.nasa.gov): the list of open natural events.

Links in the app (for example "Worldview" or a source link) open in your browser or an in-app browser, on websites we do not control.

## Optional AI service
"Ask ECHO" works offline with a built-in guide. If you enter the address of an AI service in Settings, then each time you send a question the app sends that question, recent messages in the conversation, and a summary of the active investigation (its title, topic, the date you are viewing, its written summary, its source links, and up to five of your notes) to that address, and to nowhere else. The service you point it to is run by you or someone you trust, and its own privacy policy applies. Leave the address empty to keep everything on your device.

## Children
ECHO is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date.

## Your rights
Because we do not hold your data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. If you believe a service listed above holds personal data about you, contact that service. For anything else, write to the contact address above.
