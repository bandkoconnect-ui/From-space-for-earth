TRANSPARENCY BY DESIGN

What powers ECHO?

ECHO now loads dated, real NASA GIBS MODIS true-colour daily composite imagery for the swipe comparison, alongside Esri satellite basemap tiles. MODIS imagery is coarse (about 250–500 m depending on bands) and cloud conditions can obscure details. ECHO does not run flood segmentation, compute verified change, or provide operational forecasts.
